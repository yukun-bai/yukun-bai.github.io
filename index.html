I am an M.Sc. student at Nanyang Technological University, working on embodied AI for robot manipulation. My research asks how a robot policy can see precisely (grounding-first VLA), learn quickly in the real world (sample-efficient online RL with human-in-the-loop), and remember what it has done (multi-timescale memory for long-horizon manipulation). I have hands-on experience training and deploying policies on Franka FR3 and ARX X5 dual-arm robots. Before NTU, I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from Dalian University of Technology.

Grounding-first VLA: Localize via Visual References, Then Act Grounding-first VLA：视觉引用引导的定位与动作生成

A*STAR · 2026.01 – 2026.05
Research intern研究实习 · Qwen2.5-VL-3B / PaDT-inspired Grounding / Flow-Matching DiT / LIBERO
92% → 98.3%
avg. success on four LIBERO suitesLIBERO 四套任务平均成功率
  • Autoregressive architecture. Built on starVLA with Qwen2.5-VL-3B: a dual-view dynamic vocabulary produces VRTs, and PaDT-style two-way attention predicts boxes, masks and confidences. An Affordance Token Bridge fuses references, mask-pooled and spatial features, which together with the raw VLM context condition a 16-layer flow-matching DiT that outputs 8-step × 7-D action chunks.
  • Parallel decoding. Learnable query tokens with task-role and camera-view encodings predict visual-patch reference distributions in a single VLM forward pass, replacing autoregressive decoding with differentiable soft references; offline PaDT-teacher distillation and action-alignment training cut the per-token decoding cost of grounding.
  • Results. Raised the average success rate of the existing grounding model on the four LIBERO suites from about 92% to 98.3% (≈ +6 points).
  • 自回归架构：在 starVLA 上以 Qwen2.5-VL-3B 和双视角动态词表生成 VRT，经 PaDT 式 two-way attention 预测框、掩码与置信度；新增 Affordance Token Bridge 融合引用、掩码池化及空间特征，与原始 VLM 上下文共同条件化 16 层 Flow-Matching DiT，生成 8 步 × 7 维动作块。
  • 并行优化：引入带任务角色与相机视角编码的可学习查询 Token，通过一次 VLM 前向并行预测视觉 Patch 引用分布，通过可微 soft references 替代自回归解码；设计离线 PaDT Teacher 蒸馏与动作对齐训练，降低视觉定位的逐 token 解码开销。
  • 实验结果：现有 Grounding 模型在 LIBERO 四套任务上平均成功率由约 92% 提升至 98.3%（约 +6 个百分点）。

REMA: Real-world Online RL with Explicit Manifold Alignment REMA：显式流形对齐的真机在线强化学习

PINE Lab, NTU · 2025.09 – 2026.01

  • Method. Binary sparse rewards cannot tell informative near-success failures from divergent exploration. REMA builds a reference manifold from the low-frequency DCT spectra of successful demonstrations and penalizes only off-manifold failures — a plug-and-play reward refinement that improves credit assignment in contact-rich manipulation without modifying the RL algorithm.
  • My contributions. Implemented trajectory alignment (arc-length resampling, quaternion handling), DCT manifold distance, quantile-threshold reward relabeling and EMA manifold updates; built the Franka FR3 real-robot training and evaluation system (SpaceMouse teleoperation, real-time intervention); ran HIL-SERL online training and WSRL fine-tuning on pipe-insertion and towel-folding tasks.
  • Results. In real-robot human-in-the-loop RL, autonomous success rose from 62.5% to 80.6% and the intervention rate dropped from 60.8% to 29.9%; in simulation, 1.2× faster convergence and +7.4% success; also improves WSRL fine-tuning under a 30-minute online budget.
  • 方法：二值稀疏奖励无法区分“接近成功的有效失败”与“偏离任务的发散探索”。REMA 以成功示教的低频 DCT 频谱构建参考流形，仅惩罚离流形失败，以即插即用、不改动 RL 算法的方式改善接触密集型操作中的信用分配。
  • 个人贡献：实现轨迹对齐（弧长重采样、四元数处理）、DCT 流形距离、分位数阈值奖励重标注与流形 EMA 更新；搭建 Franka FR3 真机训练评测系统（SpaceMouse 遥操作、实时介入），完成插管、叠毛巾任务的 HIL-SERL 在线训练与 WSRL 微调。
  • 结果：真机人机协同 RL 中自主成功率 62.5% → 80.6%、人工干预率 60.8% → 29.9%；仿真收敛提速 1.2 倍、成功率提升 7.4%；30 分钟在线预算下同样提升 WSRL 微调效果。
